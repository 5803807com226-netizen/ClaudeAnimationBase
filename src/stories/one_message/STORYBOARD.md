# ข้อความเดียว ทำไมทำให้เราหงุดหงิดทั้งวัน? (one message)

Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, 15 s. Silent prototype: the narration below is timing reference only (never drawn).
Retime to the real Thai narration by editing `NARRATION` and `CUE` in `story.js`; set `audio` in `config.js`.

**Logline.** One small message lands on a phone; we can't stop replaying it, until the replay is all that's left.
**World.** Warm paper by day → lavender → deep indigo "mind". Coral is the message, always; it melts into the mind's colours.
**Motif.** The coral speech bubble: phone → floating alone → multiplied around the head → melted into blobs → a spiral.
**Arc.** Neutral → irritated (the brow knits as the copies pile up) → absorbed (the mind takes over) → the loop goes on.
**Rhyme.** Opens with a coral brush wipe (the message's colour); closes with an indigo/violet one (the mind's).
