## Blockers fixed (commit `3139471`)

1. **Editing a rig had no effect on how the artwork was cut.**
   - **Cause:** each bone's region (capsule radius) was measured once, from the automatic joint guess. Moving joints by hand kept those wrong regions, so Taro's arms carried pieces of his head.
   - **Fix:** `rig_analyze.py --joints=<edited rig>` keeps the hand-placed joints and re-measures every region. The studio runs this automatically when you save joints.
   - **Result:** Taro's upper arm went from 107 → 26 px and his head from 37 → 224 px.
2. **The character drifted off the ground after an upper-body action.**
   - **Cause:** in "walk, then wave", nothing drove the legs after the walk.
   - **Fix:** the studio's quick sets now keep a leg action until the end.
   - **Result:** Taro's measured checks went from **1/6 to 5/6** (feet planted 0.44 px; one 1.67 px slide against a 1.5 px limit).
3. **The audit reported installed patches as missing.**
   - **Cause:** v2, v3 and v5 edit the same files, so checking v2 on its own fails once v5 is installed.
   - **Fix:** the audit now checks newest first, on a copy of the files, reversing each installed patch. A toy test confirmed the old check gave the wrong answer and the new one gives the right one.

## Remaining problems (not fixed: they need new features, not fixes)

- **Single-image rigs are not production quality for Taro and Dino.**
  - **Taro:** after the fixes he walks with planted feet and his head is intact. Thin slivers of the original arms still stay behind when the arms move.
  - **Dino:** a side view with a tail and stubby legs doesn't fit the human template, and the quadruped template can't be played (it crashes the engine).
  - **The intended route:** cut-out puppet parts (`tools/action/puppet.py`). Its ComfyUI driver, `tools/action/gen_character.mjs`, is referenced but was never committed.
- **`verify.mjs` measures the skeleton, not the picture.** Dino passes 5 of 6 checks while the image is visibly broken, so the frames must always be looked at too. The studio now says this on the result page.
- **Stone Friends, 17–18 s:** Taro's shocked-face sticker is cut off at the left edge of the frame, and the subtitle covers the meat and the dinosaur's mouth. These are manifest placement issues.
- **No narration voice:** the system has no text-to-speech.
