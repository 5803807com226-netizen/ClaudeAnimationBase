# Foundation kit: deep time, spread, empires, action effects

These presets come from `docs/PRESET_ROADMAP.md` (Tier 1 and the war extras) and are defined in `src/presets/epic.js`.
- **Formats:** they PASS 9:16 / 16:9 / 4:5.
- **Demo:** `tools/fixtures/plans/earth_to_ai.json`, "จากโลกกำเนิดถึงยุค AI" (42 s). Its preview sheet, with mock aircraft, is `previews/earth_to_ai_MOCKART_sheet.jpg`.
- **Test:** `node tools/aspect_test.mjs --only=map_epic,preset_blast,preset_eraTicker,preset_deepTimeline,preset_barRace`. In the cloud, add `--assets=out/mock_assets/_plan_earth_to_ai/earth_to_ai_art/`.

| Preset | Space | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `eraTicker` | screen | Shows when we are in time. `keys: [[t, ago \| {year}]]` counts through deep time in log steps, from "4,600 ล้านปีก่อน" to "ค.ศ. 1945". `names` puts an era name under the date; `era: "be"` shows พ.ศ. |
| `deepTimeline` | screen | A ruler of `eras` (years ago, coloured bands), log or linear. Its window zooms through `window: [[t, fromAgo, toAgo]]`, and a "here" marker moves through `marker: [[t, ago]]` |
| `mapBlast` | map | Explosions at a sourced place: flash, fireball, shock ring, sparks and rising smoke. `hits` gives several blasts. Each adds a warm light flash and a camera shake |
| `blast` | screen | The same explosion at (x, y) over any scene or collage |
| `mapDogfight` | map | Two artwork aircraft circle and chase, as a circle or a figure eight (`radius` in km, or `px` for a close shot), with smoke trails and ground shadows. `bursts: [[from, to]]` fire tracers with muzzle flashes and hit sparks; `down: {at}` sends the chased plane spinning down in black smoke to a crash blast |
| `mapFlow` | map | A broad tapered arrow of spread (a migration, a faith, farming, a trade) with flowing texture inside and a dated label at its head. Several layers make a branching spread |
| `mapEmpire` | map | A territory through dated `steps: [{at, units, label}]`, cross-fading with an edge pulse. The compiler checks every unit name in every step |
| `barRace` | screen | A bar-chart race from a sourced table (`years`, `series[].values`) on a dark plate. Bars swap places as values change, the year shows large, and the `source` line is printed under the chart |

## Camera
- **`mapView` keys** take `tilt` (a dutch angle in degrees) besides `center`, `zoom` and `rot`. The frame is scaled up just enough to stay covered.
- **`mapView shakes: [[at, amp, dur]]`** adds a scripted shake. `mapBlast` and `mapDogfight` also request shakes on their own.
- **A globe (`orthographic`)** now floats in space: a blue-black gradient, a faint Milky Way band, and stars in three depths that drift with the turn (parallax).
- **Motion blur** now measures real travel only. Before, the slide that keeps a flat map off the poles was read as motion and blurred the frame.

## Sound
`tools/lib/polish.mjs` places cues automatically:

| Preset | Cue |
|---|---|
| `mapBlast` / `blast` | `explosion` on every hit |
| `mapDogfight` | `whoosh` at the start, `gunfire` on every burst, `explosion` on the crash |
| `mapFlow`, `deepTimeline` | `whoosh` |
| `eraTicker` | `tick` |
| `mapEmpire`, `barRace` | `riser` |

`python tools/make_sfx.py` adds starter `explosion`, `gunfire` and `rumble` sounds. Your own `.wav` files placed in `assets/sfx/<category>/` are used too.

## Rules kept
- **Aircraft and people:** these are artwork, generated by the local ComfyUI. Prompts exclude insignia, flags, letters and hate symbols.
- **What code draws:** only light, smoke, sparks, tracers and arrows.
- **Sourced data:** places, routes, dates and figures come from sourced `data`. Approximate ones say so:
  - the migration routes are simplified;
  - the Mongol extent uses modern borders as an approximation;
  - the dogfight loop is illustrative.
